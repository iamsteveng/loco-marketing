# LocoBike Marketing

Public marketing pages for LocoBike (樂區單車): promotions, kiosk locations, and customer programs.

## Student Benefit Program

**Student Benefit Program** (學生優惠計劃):
A LocoBike offer for students who pass Student Verification.
_Avoid_: Student discount, student plan

**Student Verification**:
Proving student status by receiving a code at an email address whose domain is an Eligible Email Domain. It proves the person holds a student address, not that they are currently enrolled — some schools let graduates keep it.
_Avoid_: Student check, school verification

**Eligible School** (合資格學校):
A secondary school (中學) or post-secondary institution (專上學院) whose students can pass Student Verification. Post-secondary means offering higher diplomas, associate degrees, or degrees. Primary schools, continuing / further education schools, and the Vocational Training Council (職業訓練局) are never Eligible Schools.
_Avoid_: Participating school, partner school, included school

**Eligible Email Domain** (合資格電郵域名):
An email domain accepted by Student Verification, matched exactly (subdomains are not implied). It must identify exactly one Eligible School and nothing broader, and be issued to students rather than staff. An Eligible School may have several.
_Avoid_: School domain, school email domain

**School Application**:
A request, made through customer service, to add a school that is not yet an Eligible School.
_Avoid_: School request, nomination
